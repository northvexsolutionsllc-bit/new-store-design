04Get your preview link

Nothing goes live until you publish from Shopify admin.

Option A — Shopify CLI, logged into the gloeus store, from the repo:
shopify theme push --unpublished --path .
prints an unpublished preview URL.

Option B — GitHub integration: connect this repo to the gloeus store and pull the branch as a new unpublished theme. Then run Lighthouse mobile on home + product.

05Your 3-minute iPhone checklist

  1. Open home — hero reveals once, smoothly; reload → same, never restarts mid-scroll.
  2. Scroll end-to-end — benefits stagger, How-It-Works scrubs Apply→Wait→Wipe, testimonials glide, offer shimmers, FAQ opens.
  3. Tap Add to cart → drawer slides in → close.
  4. Product page — scroll; the sticky Add-to-cart bar appears above the safe area and adds to cart.
  5. Nothing flashes, freezes, or double-animates; text readable throughout.
